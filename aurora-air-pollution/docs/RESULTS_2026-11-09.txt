MAIN CONCLUSION
The January 2003 Aurora baseline completed 200 real-data optimizer updates,
saved best/last checkpoints and completed a held-out evaluation. This establishes
that the training and evaluation pipeline works. It does not establish a useful
air-pollution forecast advantage: PM2.5 performance is mixed and often worse than
persistence, and raw forecasts contain negative concentrations. No completed
advection-versus-baseline forecasting comparison exists in the inspected runs.

COMPLETED EXPERIMENT
Model: official 12-hour Aurora weather checkpoint extended with pollutant inputs
and outputs; ordinary full fine-tuning, no additional advection branch.
Trainable parameters: 1,256,857,440. Seed: 42.
Data: CAMS EAC4 reanalysis, global 1.5-degree 120 x 240 grid, 12-hour intervals.
Training: 1–20 January 2003 (38 valid windows).
Validation: 21–24 January 2003 (6 valid windows).
Held-out test interval: 25–31 January 2003. All dates UTC and inclusive here.
Two past states predict the next state; four microbatches per optimizer update.
Training objective: equal-channel area-weighted MAE divided by each channel's
training-period 12-hour persistence MAE, averaged across all 142 weather and
pollution channels. It is not a pollutant-only physical RMSE.
Validation objective at steps 50/100/150/200: 2.8825, 2.1317, 1.9421, 1.8197.
Best and latest checkpoints were both saved at step 200 (see training.log).
Validation selects the best checkpoint; test data are used for evaluation.

HELD-OUT EVALUATION AND ITS LIMITS
Only two forecast origins were evaluated: 25 January 2003 12:00 UTC and
26 January 2003 12:00 UTC. Requiring two input states plus a full 120-hour
rollout within the short test interval, with stride two, leaves these two cases.
The forecasts overlap heavily; they are not independent samples of seasonal skill.
Leads: 12, 24, ..., 120 hours. Metrics: area-weighted MAE, RMSE, signed bias,
and area-weighted negative-prediction fraction. Predictions are not clipped.
India means a 6–38 N, 68–98 E bounding box, not a national land-only mask.
Ground truth is EAC4 reanalysis, not independent station observations.
No unfine-tuned Aurora evaluation, confidence intervals, multiple seeds,
extreme-event analysis or broad seasonal evaluation is present.

ADVECTION STATUS
NowCastNet-inspired motion/source evolution, ADRNet-inspired latent dynamics,
and de Bezenac-inspired Gaussian transport branches are implemented. Alongside
the baseline and two fixed-transport controls, all six variants passed two real
EAC4 optimizer updates in earlier integration checks. Those trial weights were
discarded. The two-step losses are software checks, not a model ranking.
The approaches are paper-inspired adaptations, not exact paper reproductions.
No held-out superiority, new best method, or advection benefit can be claimed.
